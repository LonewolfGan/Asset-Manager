## Free Forever Plan

- **Price:** $0.00 / month ($0.00 / year)
- **Limits:** Unlimited file conversions, unlimited file size up to local browser RAM capacity (typically up to 2GB per file)
- **Billing:** No credit card required. No billing information collected.
- **Account:** No account required. No registration or login required. Immediate access without authentication walls.
- **Hidden Fees:** Zero hidden fees. No paywalls, no trial expirations, no watermarks placed on exported files.
- **Commercial Use:** Allowed for personal, commercial, academic, and enterprise workflows.
